# User Manual & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)
## Swaramayi Real Estate Marketing – Enterprise Real Estate Brokerage ERP, CRM & AI Business Operating System

---

## 1. Purpose & Governance
This document provides standard operational procedures (SOPs) for all staff members of Swaramayi Real Estate Marketing. Compliance with these procedures is enforced automatically by the ERP system.

---

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures by Role

### 2.1 Sales Executive (Agent) SOP
1. **Daily Check-In**: Open the Swaramayi Mobile App at your assigned branch or site location by 09:30 AM. Allow location services for GPS check-in with selfie face verification.
2. **Lead Response**: When a new lead is assigned, make initial contact within 15 minutes via the built-in masked calling button. Log all discussion notes in the interaction timeline.
3. **Site Visit Protocol**: Upon arriving at the site with a prospective customer, tap **"Initiate Site Visit Check-in"**. Ask the customer for the 6-digit OTP sent to their mobile phone and enter it to validate the visit.
4. **Booking Procedure**: Select the unit from the interactive property grid. Collect the token payment reference, upload customer Aadhaar & PAN photos for OCR validation, and submit for Branch Manager approval.

### 2.2 Branch Manager SOP
1. **Morning Lead Review**: Review the AI Lead Scoring queue and confirm round-robin allocations. Reassign uncontacted leads that breached the 15-minute SLA.
2. **Site Visit Validation**: Audit unverified site visit flags and approve genuine exceptions.
3. **Booking & Price Approvals**: Review provisional bookings. Verify that prices match the approved discount schedule before issuing formal allotment letters.

### 2.3 Finance Manager SOP
1. **Builder Billing**: Once a booking reaches the registration stage, trigger the system-generated builder commission invoice.
2. **Commission Payout Verification**: Verify system-calculated commission splits. Submit payout batches for Super Admin digital approval.

### 2.4 Super Admin / Executive Owner SOP
1. **Executive Dashboard**: Monitor real-time sales revenue, commission ledgers, active lead pipelines, and AI revenue forecasts.
2. **Security & Threat Center**: Monitor active sessions, review bulk export alert traps, and manage device binding permissions. Execute Emergency Lockdown if security anomalies arise.
